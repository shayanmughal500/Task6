About the Artist

Capturing the
World in White

I have spent over fifteen years wandering frozen landscapes — from the fjords of Norway to the plains of Hokkaido — searching for that perfect moment when light meets ice and the world holds its breath.

Each photograph in this collection is taken during the brief window between snowfall and thaw, when everything is still and the world feels like a secret shared between you and the cold.
